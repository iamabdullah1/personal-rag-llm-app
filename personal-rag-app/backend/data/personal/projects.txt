### Status
Live and View More available

- **Multi-Portal System:**

  - Consultant Portal (manage students & appointments)

- All portals have protected routes with role-based authentication
- Complete information management system

## Project 5: Investx - Real Estate Tokenization Platform

### Description
An innovative e-commerce/fintech platform that tokenizes real estate (apartments and land) into smaller chunks, allowing multiple buyers to invest in property with smaller portions. This democratizes real estate investment for middle-class and lower-income individuals who previously couldn't afford full property purchases.

### Technologies Used
- Next.js
- React.js
- Node.js
- Tailwind CSS
- MongoDB

### Status
Highly Developed MVP

### Key Features
- **Property Tokenization:** Apartments and land divided into purchasable tokens
- **Target Audience:** Middle class and lower-income investors
- **Multi-Role System with 4 Sections:**
  - Guest (browse properties)
  - Investor (buy tokens, track portfolio)
  - Admin (full platform control)
  - Semi-Admin (limited administrative access)
- All routes are protected with role-based authentication
- OTP (One-Time Password) verification for security
- Investment tracking and portfolio management
- Property listing and management
